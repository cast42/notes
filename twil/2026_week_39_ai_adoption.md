# TWIL — 2026 week 39 — AI adoption

## TL;DR

AI adoption was the week’s top-of-mind topic: the notes moved from **how AI changes human capability**, to **what it changes in creative work**, to **the infrastructure bet required to scale it**. The common question is not whether AI is powerful, but where human judgment, learning, and scarce resources go next.

AI first appeared as a **capability-and-learning trade-off**: tools can remove the very cognitive work that builds independent skill. That led to a **creative-work question**: even if production becomes cheaper, meaningful stories still depend on human taste and purpose.
