## TL;DR

Make thinking and progress visible, but don't confuse a polished output or a single metric with understanding. In complex systems, safety depends on interacting defenses and human adaptation—not a tidy single cause.

## Connections

Writing a research question, tracking progress and examining an incident all make assumptions visible—but each needs a different kind of evidence. Combine explicit records with alternative explanations and context, rather than treating the record itself as proof.
