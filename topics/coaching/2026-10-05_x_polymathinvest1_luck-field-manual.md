# The Luck Field Manual: increase exposure to opportunity

## TL;DR

- The attached pages frame luck as something we cannot command, but can meet more often and be better prepared to use: increase exposure, improve recognition and conversion, and remain resilient.
- The practical heuristics range from trying more small experiments and making work visible, to building weak ties, making asymmetric bets, avoiding ruin, and preserving slack.
- This connects directly to the repo's existing idea that luck's “surface area” can be increased, while adding more detail about networks, adaptability, and downside protection.

## What is useful here

The useful distinction is between **randomness** and the conditions around it. We cannot guarantee a lucky outcome. We can, however, increase the number of meaningful attempts and encounters, make our work easier to discover, build relationships across groups, and prepare enough skill and slack to act when an opening appears. Avoiding ruin matters because exposure only compounds if we remain able to take another shot.

These are heuristics, not a validated recipe. “More attempts” can become activity without learning; visibility can reward performance over substance; networking can become transactional; and optionality has a carrying cost. Prefer experiments that produce feedback, relationships with mutual value, and reversible bets whose downside is survivable.
