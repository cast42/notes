# Raw source capture

## Wikipedia — overview and edition history

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Style:_Lessons_in_Clarity_and_Grace

Title: **Style: Lessons in Clarity and Grace**

Principal author: **Joseph M. Williams** (1933–2008), with coauthoring and later posthumous revisions by Gregory G. Colomb and Joseph Bizup.

The book aims to teach people how to write clearly and gracefully. Williams was a professor of English Language and Literature at the University of Chicago. The page associates the book with the principle that “It is good to write clearly, and anyone can.”

The original book was first published in 1981 and was based on Williams’s University of Chicago course, “The Little Red Schoolhouse.” The work has gone through numerous editions and related titles. The 13th edition was published in 2021.

The Wikipedia page describes three related titles:

- **Style: Lessons in Clarity and Grace**, originally titled *Style: Ten Lessons in Clarity and Grace*, with 13 editions through 2021.
- **Style: The Basics of Clarity and Grace**, with five editions through 2014.
- **Style: Toward Clarity and Grace**, one 1990 edition reprinted in many print runs.

The page says the editions share a broad purpose: clear and graceful writing, with roughly 10–12 chapters covering different aspects of style.

## Pearson — 13th edition

Source: https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/style-lessons-in-clarity-and-grace/P200000002140

Pearson lists the 13th edition by Joseph M. Williams and Joseph Bizup. The publisher’s overview states that the book asserts that good style is a matter of making informed choices in the service of one’s readers.

The Pearson page lists ISBN-13 `9780137536603` for the 2021 edition.
