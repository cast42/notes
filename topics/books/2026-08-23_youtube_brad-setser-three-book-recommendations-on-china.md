# Brad Setser’s three book recommendations on China

## TL;DR

Brad Setser recommends one book directly about China and two broader books that supply useful analytical frameworks:

1. **The Party: The Secret World of China’s Communist Rulers** — Richard McGregor
2. **The Volatility Machine: Emerging Economies and the Threat of Financial Collapse** — Michael Pettis
3. **How to Win a Trade War: An Optimistic Guide to an Anxious Global Economy** — Soumaya Keynes and Chad P. Bown

## Why he recommends them

- **The Party** most shaped Setser’s understanding of China. Its central lesson is that modern China cannot be understood without understanding the modern Chinese Communist Party and its reach into business.
- **The Volatility Machine** is a difficult but, in Setser’s view, modern-classic framework for understanding financial vulnerabilities and how emerging markets get into trouble. He also sees it as relevant to financial structures supporting the AI buildout.
- **How to Win a Trade War** is a non-polemical guide to a world that increasingly treats trade as a source of vulnerability, not only opportunity. Setser says readers will learn from it regardless of which side of the trade debate they occupy.
