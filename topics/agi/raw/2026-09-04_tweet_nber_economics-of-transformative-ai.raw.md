# Research capture: *The Economics of Transformative AI*

## NBER announcement

On 4 September 2026, NBER announced a new book, *The Economics of
Transformative AI*. The post links to the NBER book-and-chapters page.

Source: [NBER announcement post](https://x.com/nberpubs/status/2095925839384678555)

## Official book metadata

The NBER page identifies Ajay K. Agrawal, Erik Brynjolfsson, and Anton Korinek
as editors. It lists a September 2026 publication date, a conference held on
18–19 September 2025, and the University of Chicago Press as publisher. The
book contains sixteen research studies with comments.

Source: [NBER book page](https://www.nber.org/books-and-chapters/economics-transformative-ai)

The University of Chicago Press page describes the volume as an examination of
how transformative AI may help or harm human economic progress. It groups the
chapters into foundations; markets, competition, and organisation; labour,
distribution, and human welfare; information, knowledge, and systemic risks;
and policy responses and long-term considerations.

Source: [University of Chicago Press](https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/chicago/E/bo280995206.html)

## Chapter signals

- Value of transformative AI, AI in R&D, and measurement.
- Competition, welfare, AI agents, market design, and firms.
- Worker adaptation, post-AGI work and growth, and human welfare.
- Behavioural economics, dispersed knowledge, and algorithmic science.
- Information ecosystems, public finance, and existential-risk spending.

## Synthesis

The volume's unifying frame is institutional rather than merely technical:
transformative AI may alter decision rights, market coordination, productive
power, labour's role, information systems, and the distribution of gains. It is
best read as a structured research agenda and set of analytical tools, not as a
single settled forecast.
