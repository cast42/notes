# CIA Tradecraft Primer on evidence and assumptions

## TL;DR

The CIA's March 2009 *A Tradecraft Primer* describes structured analytic techniques. The sections on information quality and assumptions inform [Check evidence quality](check_evidence_quality.md).

## Key takeaways

- Review the collection context and limitations of important information, rather than relying on the number of reports.
- Revisit assumptions that determine the conclusion, especially when circumstances change.
- Preserve qualifications and check whether withdrawn reporting affected earlier judgments.
