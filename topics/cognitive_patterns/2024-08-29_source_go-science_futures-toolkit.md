# GO-Science Futures Toolkit on scenarios and policy testing

## TL;DR

The UK Government Office for Science explains how to build scenarios and test policy options against them. Its guidance informs [Test a plan across plausible futures](test_a_plan_across_plausible_futures.md).

## Key takeaways

- Scenarios describe possible conditions rather than predict a single future.
- Compare policy options across the same conditions and identify adaptations.
- Consider perspectives beyond the team that proposed the policy.

## Reviewed material and limits

The HTML edition was updated on August 29, 2024. The reviewed sections are "Scenarios" and "Policy Stress-Testing", including the two by two scenario approach and the comparison of options across scenarios.

The source describes facilitated foresight work. The local pattern is a shorter adaptation for a defined decision. Its use does not reproduce the range of experience available in a diverse workshop. Neither scenario construction nor the number of scenarios supplies event probabilities.
