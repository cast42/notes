# Causal Inference: What If, August 2026 revision

## TL;DR

Hernan and Robins explain how causal questions depend on a specified comparison and assumptions connecting that comparison to observed data. The book informs [Examine a causal claim](examine_a_causal_claim.md).

## Key takeaways

- An association and an intervention effect are different quantities.
- A hypothetical target trial helps specify the intended comparison.
- Adjustment requires causal assumptions; including every measured variable can introduce bias.
